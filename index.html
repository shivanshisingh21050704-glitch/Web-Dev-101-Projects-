01 / My story

A little logic.
A little feeling.
A lot of curiosity.

“I'm not trying to have everything figured out. I'm trying to keep becoming someone I'm proud of.”
— a thought that feels like me

I'm Shivanshi Singh, currently beginning my next chapter as a student at Scalar School of Technology in Bengaluru. I'm excited by technology, but I don't want my world to be limited to a screen or a syllabus.

I'm working on my programming foundations, starting with Python, and building toward stronger skills in software engineering, AI, and cybersecurity. I like the idea of understanding how things work — and then making something useful out of that understanding.

There's another side of me that has always mattered: art, books, and writing things down. I studied Fine Arts and love painting; I also keep a personal journal and gravitate toward literature that makes you pause and think.

I'm ambitious, sometimes a little all-over-the-place, and still learning how to turn big goals into small, consistent steps. This portfolio is a snapshot of that process — not a finished story.
